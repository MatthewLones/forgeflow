Why Every Developer Should Learn to Write

Write a blog post arguing that writing skills are the most underrated superpower for software engineers. Cover these angles:

1. Code reviews are writing. Pull request descriptions, commit messages, and inline comments are all forms of technical writing that directly impact team velocity.

2. Documentation is a product. Good docs reduce support burden, onboard new hires faster, and make open-source projects succeed or fail.

3. Clear thinking requires clear writing. The act of writing forces you to organize your thoughts — if you can't explain a system in prose, you probably don't fully understand it.

4. Career leverage. Engineers who can write well get promoted faster, land better roles, and build personal brands through blog posts and conference talks.

Target audience: mid-career software engineers (3-7 years experience) who are technically strong but haven't invested in writing skills. Tone should be conversational and persuasive, not preachy.
